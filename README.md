Sunset Keys is an open-world crime game set in Marisol Bay, a fictional coastal city with a beach, a marina, a lighthouse and a downtown full of traffic. Steal a car, run four story missions (Borrowed Keys, Sunset Delivery, Heat Wave, Keys to the City), outrun the Marisol Bay police as the wanted level climbs, earn cash from pickups and taxi fares, and keep the city yours after the story ends in free roam. The whole game runs in the browser with no build step and no assets to download: every sound is synthesized with WebAudio, every frame is drawn on a canvas, and the day turns to dusk and night over the city as you play.

## Run it

```sh
npx tsx serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080/game/
```

Controls: WASD or arrows to move and drive, E to steal or enter a car, SPACE to punch, SHIFT for the handbrake, M to mute, ENTER to start.
